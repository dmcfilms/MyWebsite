    Dakar perspective. Global visual language.

    DMC Films is led by Djim Mouhameth Cisse, a motion designer, VFX artist and video editor based in Dakar, Senegal. The studio focuses on storytelling, rhythm, design and cinematic visuals.

    What we do

    • Motion DesignAnimated systems, titles, brand visuals and kinetic storytelling.
    • VFXCompositing, cleanup, screen replacements and invisible polish.
    • Video EditingSharp narrative pacing for campaigns, interviews and social content.
    • CommercialsPremium visual language for brands, launches and campaign films.
    • InterviewsClean, human and cinematic interview production and editing.
    • 3D AnimationProduct shots, animated environments and design-led scenes.
    • NGO / ExplainersAccessible stories that translate complex ideas into clear moving images.

    How we work

    • DiscoverUnderstand the audience, message, references and production needs.
    • ConceptShape the core idea, visual direction and creative treatment.
    • CreateBuild the edit, design frames, animate and refine the details.
    • DeliverExport polished files for web, broadcast and social platforms.
